Engineering Philosophy & Journey

I hold a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree in Computer Science & Statistics from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN). This dual background in computational systems and statistical science shapes how I engineer software: I design for edge cases, optimize data structures, test systematically, and measure system performance empirically.

Over the past 3+ years, I have architected production backends with Node.js, NestJS, and Python, completed all 6 stages of the HNG i14 QA Engineering track (exploratory testing, test case design, Postman API testing, Pytest automation, GitHub Actions CI/CD pipelines, and Agile team QA), and designed data-driven platforms parsing natural language queries into SQL.

As a technical instructor at Aptech and FutureLabs, I have mentored aspiring developers in full-stack web development and software engineering. Teaching demands absolute clarity, structured communication, and deep problem-solving skills — qualities I bring directly to cross-functional engineering teams.
